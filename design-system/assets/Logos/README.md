# Logos

**Provenance:** these SVGs were traced (alpha-mask contour → Bézier/line paths, IoU ≈ 0.98 against the source raster) from the official 6000 px PNG artwork because the IPTrust asset library could not be reached. They are a stopgap: once the designer's original SVGs are available from IPTrust, replace these files and keep the names.

VANAHOM wordmark, vectorised from the official 6000 px artwork (red #B12137, grey #D9D9D9). All share one path; viewBox 1000 × 155.81 (squares 1000 × 1000, wordmark at 81.4% width, centred). `<img>` can't inherit colour, so each file has its ink baked in:

- `vanahom-wordmark-red.svg` — ink `hongtu-red` #B12137. Primary, on paper and mist.
- `vanahom-wordmark-white.svg` — ink `paper` #FFFFFF. On hongtu-red and dark photography.
- `vanahom-wordmark-grey.svg` — ink `logo-grey` #D9D9D9. On hongtu-red banners and quiet contexts; not on white (1.41:1).
- `vanahom-wordmark-ink.svg` — ink `ink` #211B1C. Single-colour print.
- `vanahom-square-red.svg`, `vanahom-square-grey.svg` — square tiles for avatars and social; transparent ground.

Clear space: one V-height on every side. Never recolour, stretch, rotate or retypeset.
