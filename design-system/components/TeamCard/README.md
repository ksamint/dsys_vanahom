# TeamCard

A team member summary inside a chamfered hongtu-red frame (top-left and bottom-right corners cut).

**Consumer provides:** name as 中文名 + English NAME (family name in capitals), role (中文 / EN abbreviation), and 2–4 lines of concrete record.

- Role in `hongtu-red-deep`, bio in ink-soft at 13px.
- Wrap cards in `.vh-team-grid`: 3-up on wide screens, 2-up on tablets, 1-up on phones (this system's choice; the deck is fixed 16:9).
- Lead with named outcomes and numbers that are already public; never unpublished financials.
